      Playback

      Who can queue songs

      Pick the roles allowed to use !play in each server. A server with no roles picked is open to everyone. Members with "Manage Server" or the DJ role can always queue.

      YouTube

      YouTube increasingly blocks servers ("Sign in to confirm you're not a bot"). Signing the bot in to a Google account, together with the bundled PO-token provider, keeps playback reliable. Use a secondary Google account: automated use can get an account flagged.

      The sign-in browser container isn't set up, so "Sign in to YouTube" is unavailable. Add the youtube-login service from the project's docker-compose.yml, or paste cookies manually below.

      Advanced: paste cookies manually
      1. Open a private/incognito window and sign in to youtube.com with the account the bot should use.
      2. In that same tab, export cookies for youtube.com with a "cookies.txt" extension (Netscape format), or Cookie-Editor's JSON export.
      3. Close the private window without signing out, so the browser doesn't rotate the session.
      4. Paste the file contents below (or choose the file) and press Save cookies.

      Spotify
      
          
      
            Spotify does not let third-party apps stream its audio, so Spotify links are read for their track list and each
            song is played from its best YouTube match. Public playlists, albums, tracks and artists work even without
            credentials. Connect an account to load your own private playlists and Liked Songs.
